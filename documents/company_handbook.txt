SECTION 2: LEAVE POLICY
------------------------
2.1 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave
accrues at the rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused leave can be carried forward up to
a maximum of 10 days to the next calendar year. Any excess leave beyond 10 days will
lapse at the end of December.

2.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried
forward to the next year.

2.3 Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 6 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be
taken for more than 3 consecutive days. It cannot be carried forward or encashed.

2.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave as per the Maternity Benefit
  Act, 2017. Applicable after 80 days of service.
- Paternity Leave: 10 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of
  the child's birth.

2.5 Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the NEOSTATS HR portal (hr.neostats.com)
at least 48 hours in advance (except emergency sick leave). Manager approval is required
for all leave types. Leave during project critical phases may be restricted.

SECTION 3: ONBOARDING PROCESS
------------------------------
3.1 Pre-Joining
New employees receive a welcome email 1 week before joining. The email contains:
- Joining instructions and reporting location
- Documents required on Day 1
- IT equipment request form
- Buddy assignment (senior colleague assigned to help you settle in)

3.2 Day 1 Orientation
- 9:00 AM: Report to HR at the Bangalore office (3rd floor, Tech Tower, Koramangala)
- 9:30 AM: Welcome session with HR team
- 10:00 AM: IT setup — laptop, corporate email (name@neostats.com), access cards
- 11:00 AM: Office tour and team introductions
- 12:30 PM: Lunch with your buddy and team
- 2:00 PM: Policy and compliance briefing
- 4:00 PM: Meet your manager and get your 30-60-90 day plan

3.3 First 30 Days (Learning Phase)
- Complete all mandatory e-learning courses on the NEOSTATS Learning Platform
- Shadow senior team members on active projects
- Set up your development environment with the Engineering Starter Kit
- Attend weekly 1-on-1 with your manager

3.4 First 60 Days (Integration Phase)
- Take ownership of at least one module or task
- Complete the NEOSTATS Product Certification (internal)
- Participate in sprint planning and team stand-ups

3.5 First 90 Days (Contribution Phase)
- Deliver your first project milestone
- Complete your probation review with HR and manager
- Receive access to advanced tools and systems

SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------------------
4.1 Workplace Respect
NEOSTATS is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
workplace. Discrimination based on gender, race, religion, caste, or disability is
strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary action.

4.2 Data Confidentiality
Employees must not share client data, product roadmaps, or proprietary algorithms
with external parties. Violations are subject to legal action under NDA agreements
signed at joining.

4.3 Social Media Policy
Employees may not post confidential business information on social media. Personal
opinions shared online must clearly state they are personal and not those of NEOSTATS.

SECTION 5: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
--------------------------------------
5.1 Salary Structure
- Basic Salary: 40% of CTC
- HRA (House Rent Allowance): 20% of Basic Salary
- Special Allowance: 30% of CTC
- PF Contribution: 12% of Basic (both employee and employer)

5.2 Performance Bonus
- Annual appraisal cycle runs January–December
- Bonus payout in March based on individual and company performance
- Rating scale: Exceeds Expectations (15-20%), Meets Expectations (8-12%), Below (0-5%)

5.3 Additional Benefits
- Health Insurance: Coverage of ₹5,00,000 for employee + 2 dependents (family floater)
- Term Life Insurance: 3x CTC
- Gratuity: As per Payment of Gratuity Act (after 5 years of service)
- Flexible Work-From-Home: Up to 2 days per week (subject to manager approval)
- Internet Reimbursement: ₹1,000/month for WFH days
- Learning Budget: ₹25,000/year for external courses, certifications, conferences

SECTION 6: WORK HOURS AND REMOTE WORK
---------------------------------------
6.1 Standard Work Hours
Official working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, Monday to Friday.
Flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM are permitted with manager approval.
Core hours where all employees must be available: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST.

6.2 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Remote work requires:
- Stable internet connection (minimum 20 Mbps)
- Availability on Slack and video calls during core hours
- Prior intimation to manager by 9:00 AM on WFH days
Special WFH permissions may be granted for health reasons with a medical certificate.

SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------
7.1 Goal Setting
At the beginning of each year, employees set SMART goals in alignment with department
OKRs. Goals are documented in the NEOSTATS Performance Portal.

7.2 Mid-Year Review
A mid-year check-in is conducted in June–July to assess progress and adjust goals.

7.3 Annual Appraisal
- Conducted in January for the previous calendar year
- Self-assessment, manager assessment, and optional 360-degree feedback
- Results shared by end of February; salary revisions effective April 1

SECTION 8: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
--------------------------------
If an employee has a workplace grievance, they should:
1. First discuss informally with their immediate manager.
2. If unresolved, escalate to the HR Business Partner (HRBP).
3. File a formal complaint at grievance@neostats.com.
4. The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) will resolve within 30 working days.
All grievances are handled with strict confidentiality.

SECTION 9: SEPARATION POLICY
------------------------------
9.1 Resignation
Employees must serve a notice period of:
- Junior (0-2 years): 30 days
- Mid-level (2-5 years): 45 days
- Senior (5+ years): 60 days

9.2 Full & Final Settlement
Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
Components: pending salary, leave encashment, gratuity (if eligible), PF withdrawal.
